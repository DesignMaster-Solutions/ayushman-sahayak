                      This appeal, filed under Section 41 of the Consumer Protection Act

               2019 (hereinafter called "the Act" for short) is directed against order dated

               06/03/2020      passed      by     District     Consumer          Disputes   Redressal

               Commission, Raigarh (hereinafter referred to as "District Commission" for

               short), in Complaint Case No.CC/2020/494, whereby the complaint filed

               by the respondent herein alleging deficiency in service against the

               appellant herein in not refunding the amount wrongly collected for her

               treatment, was allowed and the appellant / opposite party was directed to

               pay within a month Rs.14,700/- (Fourteen Thousand Seven Hundred),

               compensation for mental agony Rs.3,000/- (Three Thousand) and cost of

               litigation Rs.2,000/- (Two Thousand) failing which the entire amount was

               2.      In nutshell the facts of the case as per the complaint are that on

               18.04.2019 the respondent / complainant went to the appellant / opposite

               party hospital for treatment of large abscess. The appellant / opposite

               party assured that treatment will be covered under Ayushman Card and

               admitted the respondent / complainant but later on they demanded cash

               amount and said the treatment will not be covered under Ayushman

               Card. For treatment and medicines Rs.16,200/- cash along with medicines

               separately were taken and after concession bill of Rs.14,700/- was given.

               Thus by misrepresentation the appellant / opposite party admitted the

               respondent / complainant in their Hospital and thereafter on the saying

               that only on deposit of cash the treatment will be done, they collected cash

               from the respondent/ complainant, which amounts to deficiency in

               service.     When the respondent / complainant asked on the toll free

               number of the Ayushman Card „104‟ then she came to know that

               treatment given to her was covered under the Ayushman Card. She

               complained about the matter to the CMO Officer, the matter was

               investigated and it was found that the Hospital Manager has done wrong

               with the respondent / complainant and instruction for refund of collected

               amount was given, but the said amount was not refunded hence legal

               notice was sent, which was not replied nor the amount was refunded then

               consumer complaint was filed seeking direction to the appellant /

               opposite party Hospital for refund of Rs.14,700/- with compensation for

               mental agony Rs.10,000/- and cost of legal notice Rs.2,000/- with cost of

               litigation along with interest @ 12% p.a.

               3.      The appellant / opposite party in their written version refuted the

               allegation of deficiency in service and stated that the respondent /

               complainant undergone treatment of abscess. She was not told that the

               treatment will not be covered under Ayushman Card. Cash amount was

               not demanded. It was further averred that the respondent / complainant

               wanted to stay in AC room during treatment, but the charges of AC room

               was not covered by the Government under Ayushman Card/Smart Card.

               She underwent the treatment while staying in AC room.                           Religare

               Insurance Company was appointed by the Government for Ayushman

               Card, whose representative sits in the Hospital, but the respondent /

               complainant        did    not    complete      the    formalities   before    the    said

               representative of the insurance company. The respondent / complainant

               complained to the Chief Medical Officer Raigarh, who sent Nodal Officer

               Raipur to investigate the matter. The Chief Medical Officer has not

               ordered to refund the amount. No copy of any such order has been

               brought on record. It was further averred that Rs.14,700/- has been spent

               in the treatment which includes medicines and operation. Religare

               Insurance Company has provided Ayushman Card/Smart Card and is

               necessary party in this case. The complaint is false and fabricated, which

               needs to be dismissed with cost.

               4.      Learned       District    Commission         after    consideration    of    rival

               contentions of both parties allowed the complaint and directed the

               appellant/ opposite party to pay the respondent /complainant the

               amount as aforesaid in paragraph No.1.

               5.      We have heard arguments of both parties and perused the record.

               6.       Before us the learned counsel for the appellant / opposite party

               Hospital has mainly argued that the expenditure of AC room is not

               covered under the Ayushman Card as per the scheme of the Government,

               hence the amount was charged and in doing so no deficiency in service

               was committed, the impugned order is erroneous and needs to be set

               aside. Whereas learned counsel for the respondent has vehemently argued

               that the respondent/ complainant did not stay in AC room. In fact she

               stayed in general ward and underwent treatment. He further argued that

               the treatment given to the respondent /complainant was covered under

               the Ayushman Card and unnecessarily the amount was charged, which

               has rightly been ordered to be refunded by the learned District

               Commission in the impugned order. The impugned order is just and

               proper and does not call for nay interference.

               7.      The entire dispute hinges around the fact that whether the

               respondent / complainant stayed in AC room and if yes then how much

               amount was charged for stay in AC room, but to our surprise when we

               asked the learned counsel for the appellant as to how much amount was

               charged for stay in AC room and whether such bill is brought on record,

               the appellant‟s counsel was unable to lay hand on any document to show

               that as to how much amount was charged for stay in AC room. Even there

               is nothing to show that in which ward/ room the respondent /

               complainant was admitted. If the respondent / complainant stayed in AC

               room and any amount was charged which is the only ground for not

               providing her benefit of Ayushman Card, the appellant/ opposite party

               ought to have filed some document to prove that in which room the

               respondent / complainant stayed and how much amount was charged.

               8.      So far as the argument of learned counsel for the appellant/

               opposite party that the ailment for which the respondent / complainant

               underwent treatment was not covered under the scheme of Ayushman

               Card is concerned, there is no evidence brought on record by the

               appellant / opposite party to prove this argument. Even on specific query

               complainant and treatment expenses of which is under consideration, was

               not covered under the Ayushman Card scheme, he failed to show any

               such document. Hence, this argument of learned counsel for the appellant

               / opposite party also has no substance and cannot be believed.

               9.      The respondent / complainant has filed copy of investigation

               report of Chief Medical & Health Officer, Raigarh sent to the Director

               Health Services cum Chief Executive Officer, Ayushman Bharat -

               Pradhanmantri Jan Arogya Yojana evam Mukhyamantri Swasthya Beema

               Yojana, Raipur (C.G.) in which appellant/ opposite party was found at

               fault in the matter of the respondent /complainant as well as five more

               patients and it was reported that the JMJ Hospital is not following the

               guidelines of the scheme, hence necessary action be taken against them.

               This report itself is sufficient to prove the appellant / opposite party

               guilty of deficiency in service in not providing benefit of the Ayushman

               Card to the respondent / complainant and collecting Rs.14,700/- for her

               view that this appeal has no substance it is liable to be and is hereby

               Commission. No order as to cost of this appeal. However, it is made clear
