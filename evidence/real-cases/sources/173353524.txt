                       6.      Final arguments heard and perused the record as well as the

                       7.      Having considered the arguments advanced by learned counsels

                       for both parties and considering the matter from all angles in our view the

                       core disputes involved in this matter are whether even being eligible to

                       get benefit under the MSBY scheme the opposite party did not provide

                       such benefit to the complainant and whether the opposite party has

                       committed unfair trade practice and deficiency in service in charging

                       more amount than the approved package by the Government under

                       MSBY scheme for the knee replacement operation ?

                       9.      Both the parties have made their rival contentions on the reason of

                       discharge from the Hospital. The complainant alleged that he was ready

                       to stay in the hospital till 28.11.2018, whereas the opposite party

                       contended that the complainant himself forced Dr. Saxena to discharge

                       him from the Hospital to avoid the unnecessary expenses, as a result of

                       which he was discharged on 26.11.2018. But the fact remains is that the

                       complainant was discharged on 26.11.2018 on insisting for not doing knee

                       replacement operation of his left knee and the preauthorization under

                       MSBY was cancelled on 29.11.2018. Thus, it cannot be said that because of

                       acts of the opposite party, benefit under the MSBY scheme could not be

                       provided to the complainant. In fact the preauthorization was approved

                       for Bilateral Total Knee Replacement whereas only after undergoing knee

                       replacement surgery of right knee the complainant admittedly shown his

                       adamant for not doing knee replacement surgery of his left knee and

                       ultimately discharged on 26.11.2018 due to which his preauthorization

                       was cancelled. The opposite party Hospital had to intimate about the

                       adamant of the complainant for not undergoing knee replacement surgery

                       of his left knee and his discharge from the Hospital. Had such intimation

                       10.     So far as the question of charging excess amount is concerned, the

                       complainant only on the basis of list of approved package for various

                       treatment/ procedures by the Government has alleged that package of

                       total knee replacement was of Rs.80,000/- only whereas the opposite party

                       has charged Rs.1,30,000/-. It is an admitted position that before

                       undergoing the surgery Dr. Saxena explained about the treatment and

                       package of Rs.1,30,000/- and in such situation only on the basis of list of

                       approved package it cannot be said that any amount was obtained in

                       excess. The approved rate by the Government was for the purpose of

                       coverage of insurance under the MSBY scheme. Learned District

                       Commission has arrived at a wrong conclusion that the opposite party

                       collected excess amount from the complainant. In our considered view the

                       impugned order is not justified in the facts and circumstances of the case,

                       hence the same deserves to be set aside.

                       11.     Accordingly, with the foregoing discussion, we allow the appeal of

                       the opposite party i.e. Appeal No.FA/24/68 and set aside the impugned

                       order. Appeal No.FA/24/62 filed by the complainant for enhancement of

                       award is dismissed accordingly. Parties are left to bear their own cost.

                       Original of this order be kept in the record of Appeal No.FA/24/62 and a
