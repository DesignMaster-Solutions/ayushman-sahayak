                                     C528 No.71 of 2024
                                     Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.

                                     1.    Mr. Bhupesh Kandpal, learned
                                     counsel for the applicant.

                                     2.    By      the    instant      Criminal
                                     Miscellaneous      Application   preferred
                                     under Section 528 of the Act No. 46 of
                                     2023,     the    present    applicant    is
                                     challenging the proceeding of Criminal
                                     Case No.3532 of 2023, "Dr. Amit Deol
                                     versus Mohit Singh Bisht @ Madan
                                     Singh, wherein the present applicant
                                     has been summoned for the offence
                                     punishable under Section 500 of IPC
                                     pending in the Court of IInd Additional
                                     Chief Judicial Magistrate, Haldwani,
                                     District Nainital.

                                     3.    The brief facts of the case are that
                                     the father of the applicant was the
                                     AYUSHMAN Card holder and due to
                                     some head injury, he was admitted to
                                     Ranikhet Government Hospital on
                                     06.04.2023, where he was admitted for
                                     his diagnostic tests and on 10.04.2023,
                                     applicant's father was diagnosed by one
                                     Doctor Puneet Agarwal in Agarwal Clinic
                                     and Nursing Home, who said that
                                     everything is normal and there would be
                                     no harm and the patient is alright. On
                                     11.04.2023, the applicant's father
                                     complained for headache and he was
                                     taken to the Doctor again who advised
                                     for CT Scan, which was done on
                                     11.04.2023 and after examining the
                                     report of CT Scan, the Doctor pointed
                                     out that there is a hidden injury in the
 head and advised him to contact a
Neurosurgeon.       Subsequently,      the
applicant's father was admitted to the
Pride Hospital, Mukhani, Haldwani,
where the respondent No.2 checked him
and said that there is a clot in the brain
of the patient. Unfortunately, applicant's
father died on 02.05.2023 during
treatment. It is submitted that despite
the    fact   that   the    patient   was
AYUSHMAN Card Holder, the applicant
was instructed to get medicine from a
particular medical shop and at about
Rupees Fifty Thousand expenses were
incurred by the present applicant.

4.    Being annoyed with the conduct of
respondent-complainant, the applicant
has published his own comments on
YouTube as well as made a complaint to
the District Magistrate. Thereafter, the
District    Magistrate   constituted     a
committee of three members and the
said committee submits its report on
19.07.2023 by observing that despite
the AYUSHMAN Card Holder, the
benefit, which has to be given to the
patient, that has not been given and a
recommendation was made against
respondent-complainant for initiating a
proceeding under the Provisions of
Government Conduct Rules. The Said
report dated 19.07.2023 is on record.
Thereafter, the respondent complainant
was transferred to B.D. Pandey Hospital,
Nainital.

5.    It is submitted by learned counsel
for the applicant that being prejudice
with the complaint made by present
applicant, the respondent-complainant
 filed a complaint for the offence
punishable under Section 500 and 501
of IPC. On the said complaint, the
statements     were    recorded     under
Sections 200 and 202 Cr.P.C, and,
thereafter, the applicant has been
summoned by the Additional Chief
Judicial Magistrate, Haldwani by order
dated 28.05.2024 for the offence
punishable under Sections 500 of IPC.

6.    Being     aggrieved     with      the
summoning order dated 28.05.2024, the
present application has been preferred.

7.    Learned counsel for the applicant
submits that the proceeding as initiated
on the complaint of respondent
complainant is misconceived and there
are no ingredients which constitute an
offence punishable under Section 500 of
IPC.

8.    Learned counsel for the applicant
also submits that the allegations as
alleged in the complaint falls in the first,
ninth and tenth exception of Section
499 of IPC, which are being reproduced
herein below:

First Exception.-- Imputation of truth
which public good requires to be made
or published.-- It is not defamation to
impute     anything    which     is    true
concerning any person, if it be for the
public good that the imputation should
be made or published. Whether or not it
is for the public good is a question of
fact.

Ninth Exception.-- Imputation made in
good faith by person for protection of his
or other's interests.-- It is not
defamation to make an imputation on
 the character of another provided that
the imputation be made in good faith for
the protection of the interests of the
person making it, or of any other
person, or for the public good.
Tenth Exception.-- Caution intended
for good of person to whom conveyed or
for public good.-- It is not defamation to
convey a caution, in good faith, to one
person against another, provided that
such caution be intended for the good of
the person to whom it is conveyed, or of
some person in whom that person is
interested, or for the public good.

9.    All     these    aspects    required
deliberations for which the counter-
affidavit is necessary.

10. Issue notice to the respondent.

11. Learned counsel for the applicant
shall take steps to serve respondent
within a week returnable within three
weeks.

12. Put up this matter on 27.08.2024.

13. After taking into consideration the
report submitted by the three members'
committee constituted by the District
Magistrate dated 19.07.2023, it appears
that the benefits, as admissible, were
not given to the patient (deceased) who
was the father of the applicant was the
AYUSHMAN Card            Holder and a
recommendation was made to initiate
proceeding against respondent No.2
under the provision of Government
Servant Conduct Rules this Court is of
the view that the matter is required to
be deep scrutiny and deliberation.

14. Accordingly, till next date of listing,
further proceeding of Criminal Case
 No.3532 of 2023, "Dr. Amit Deol versus
Mohit Singh Bisht @ Madan Singh,
wherein the present applicant has been
summoned for the offence punishable
under Section 500 of IPC pending in the
Court of IInd Additional Chief Judicial
Magistrate, Haldwani, District Nainital,
shall remain stayed.
