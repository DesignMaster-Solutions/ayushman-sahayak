        The High Court Of Madhya Pradesh

(DEEPANSHU KUMAR PAYASI Vs SHRI S. VISHWANATHAN CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AYUSHMAN
                         BHARAT NIRAYAMAN AND OTHERS)

Jabalpur, Dated : 02-09-2021
      Heard through Video Conferencing.

      Per : Vijay Kumar Shukla, J.

      Shri Gudda Singh Uddey, learned counsel for the petitioner.
      The present contempt petition has been filed under Article 215 of the
Constitution of India read with Section 12 of the Contempt of Court Act,

alleging non compliance of the order dated 19.4.2021 passed in Suo Motu
Writ Petition No.8914/2020 and further a direction has been prayed that as
per the scheme, namely, "Ayushman Bharat Yojna" to grant the relief to the
extent of Rs.4 Lacs which has been incurred by the petitioner towards
expenses in his treatment due to Covid-19 pandemic.

      This Court had issued a general direction by order dated 19.4.2021
with regard to Covid-19 treatment under the Ayushman Bharat Yojana, which
is quoted as under :-

          "(xvi) The State Government should work out the modalities
          for ensuring that patients from Below Poverty Line families
          having BPL Cards under Deendayal Antyodaya Upchar Yojana
          and those having Ayushman Cards and CGHS coverage
          facilities are not dishonoured by the Hospitals/ Nursing
          Homes if they are approved for their treatment."

      The petitioner submits that he is an Ayushman Card holder and falling
under the Ayushman Bharat Niramayam beneficiary category. The petitioner
being suspected and confirmed COVID-19 was admitted in M.P. Birla
Hospital, Satna on 8.5.2021 and discharged on 18.5.2021. The total expenses
he was required to spend was Rs. 4 lacs.

        The respondent No.2 did not give the benefit of the Scheme and
charged the amount from the petitioner. The petitioner filed complaint before
the concerned authority but no action has been taken thereon.

      It is further submitted that the respondent No.2, M.P. Birla Hospital,
Satna is empanelled and approved for Covid-19 treatment under the
                                                                           2                            CONC-1638-2021
                                       Ayushman Bharat Yojana but it is not accepting the Ayushman Card and the
                                       patients from Below Poverty Line having BPL Cards under the Deendayal
                                       Antyodaya Upchar Yojana are also not being provided treatment under the
                                       Scheme by approved hospitals.

                                               Thus, the petitioner has approached this Court for deliberate and willful

                                       non compliance of this Court's order dated 19.4.2021 passed in suo motu
                                       W.P. No.8914/2020.

                                               Considering the aforesaid grievance of the petitioner, we deem it
                                       proper to dispose of the contempt petition directing the respondent No.1, the
                                       Chief Executive Officer, Ayushman Bharat (Nirayaman), JP Hospital
                                       Campus, Bhopal, to make an enquiry on the complaint of the petitioner and
                                       take action on the same in accordance with law, within a period of two
                                       months from the date of communication of the order passed today after
                                       affording opportunity of hearing to the respondent No.2.

                                               With the aforesaid observation, this contempt petition is disposed of.
